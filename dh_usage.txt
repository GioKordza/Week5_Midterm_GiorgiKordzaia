Diffie-Hellman is a cryptographic algorithm used to securely exchange encryption keys over an insecure channel. 
It is commonly used in protocols like TLS (for secure websites), VPNs, and secure messaging apps like Signal. 
The main benefit is that the shared secret is never transmitted; instead, each side computes it independently. 
This makes it very hard for attackers to intercept or guess the key, even if they capture the traffic. 
This key exchange method is essential for maintaining confidentiality in online communications.
